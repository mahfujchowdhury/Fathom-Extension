Fathom Extension installed

One quick thing

Pin Fathom to your toolbar

Fathom is installed. Pin it and every page you visit gets its tool dock one click away — no menus, no hunting.

  1. 1
    Open the extensions menu

    Click the puzzle-piece icon at the top-right of Chrome, next to the address bar.

  2. 2
    Find Fathom

    It is in the list of installed extensions, sitting beside the other ones you have.

  3. 3
    Pin it

    Click the pin beside Fathom. The icon stays on your toolbar until you unpin it.

Shortcut: Ctrl+Shift+Y (Cmd+Shift+Y on a Mac) opens the dock even when the icon is not pinned.
